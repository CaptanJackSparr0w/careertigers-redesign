What is CareerTigers?

A career-services platform that combines AI career tools, expert coaching and job-readiness programs to help campus learners move from learning to earning.

Who is CareerTigers for?

Active learners and alumni. Placement support may require final-term eligibility and academic standing.

How do I register?

Click “Get Started”, fill in your details and you’ll get access to the CareerTigers portal where all tools live.

What should I fill in my profile?

Your education, skills, target roles, preferred locations and your latest resume — the more complete it is, the better your job matches.
